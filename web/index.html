Talking Tree

Each ring is a year of growth from a Colorado Ponderosa Pine. Touch and hold a ring to hear it: years of faster growth play faster and brighter.

Press Start, then touch a ring.

Sound settings

Originally built in Max/MSP with an Arduino and capacitive sensors, ATLS 3300, CU Boulder, Spring 2019, by Alan Tett and Abby Rinerson. Rebuilt for the web in 2026 with Web Audio.
